Established in 2025, Sneha Mitra Estates is a trusted real estate company based in Vizianagaram and Visakhapatnam, known for delivering quality projects with transparency, reliability, and customer satisfaction. With a strong commitment to excellence, we have successfully developed several landmark layouts and premium open plot projects in strategic locations.

At Sneha Mitra Estates, we believe that every investment should be safe, secure, and future-ready. As trusted verified plot developers, we offer only VMRDA & RERA approved projects with 100% clear title plots, ensuring complete legal clarity and peace of mind for our customers.

Our success is driven by the visionary leadership and 20+ years of experience of our Managing Partners: Barukula Paramesh, Grandhi Srinivasarao, Peela Hari Kumar & Kolipaka Ramarao. Their deep understanding of the real estate industry and customer-focused approach have helped Sneha Mitra Estates earn the trust of hundreds of happy customers.

With modern infrastructure, wide roads, and quality civic amenities, our projects are designed to provide excellent value and long-term growth. Whether you are a first-time buyer, an NRI, or an investor looking for premium open plots and secure investments with high future returns, Sneha Mitra Estates is your trusted real estate partner.

Our Head Office is located in Visakhapatnam, with branch offices in Vizianagaram, Gajapathinagaram, Bobbili, Saluru and Parvathipuram, allowing us to serve customers efficiently across the region.
